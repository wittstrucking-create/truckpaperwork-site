IFTA Quick Card — 2026

One-page reminder for 1–5 truck ops. Fill blanks each quarter. Not tax advice — verify with your base jurisdiction before filing.

Write these down every quarter

Quarterly due months (typical)

QuarterTravel periodReturn usually due
Q1Jan 1 – Mar 31April 30
Q2Apr 1 – Jun 30July 31
Q3Jul 1 – Sep 30October 31
Q4Oct 1 – Dec 31January 31

If the due date falls on a weekend/holiday, use your base state’s next business day. Confirm your carrier’s calendar.

Before you file — quick check

  • Every jurisdiction with miles has a matching fuel figure (or a clear “bought elsewhere” note).
  • Receipts cover the gallons you claim; void / duplicate tickets pulled out.
  • MPG looks sane for this truck (wild swings → fix logs before you pay).
  • Unit #, plate, and period match what your base jurisdiction expects.

Not tax advice. This card is a planning aid only. Official rates and return rules come from IFTA / your base jurisdiction — not this page.
